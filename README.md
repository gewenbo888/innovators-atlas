# The Innovator Atlas | 创新者图谱

A bilingual single-page atlas of innovation across history, science, technology, and business. Eight deep profiles, four frameworks, an interactive mindset quiz, an innovation timeline from stone tools to AGI, and a Human-vs-AI section.

## Sections

1. What is an Innovator? — invention vs innovation, four types
2. Innovator Profiles — 8 deep dives (Da Vinci, Tesla, Curie, Edison, Lovelace, Hopper, Jobs, Musk) with background, achievements, failures, thinking style
3. Innovation Frameworks — Design Thinking, First Principles, Systems Thinking, Experimentation
4. Innovation Timeline — 14 milestones from 3.3 Mya stone tools to 2026 frontier AI
5. Industries & Breakthroughs — AI, Robotics, Energy, Medicine, Communication, Blockchain
6. Traits of Innovators — five threads
7. Innovation Process — 5-step diagram
8. Innovation vs Execution — Xerox PARC + Friendster case studies
9. **Innovation Mindset Quiz** — 5 questions, ranks you as one of 7 innovator archetypes
10. Human vs AI Innovators — three-substrate comparison
11. Future of Innovation — three plausible paths

Plus: hover-tooltips, dark/light theme toggle, bilingual EN ↔ 中文 with seamless switching, mobile-responsive.

## Disclaimer

Sources include Walter Isaacson's biographies, Susan Quinn's *Marie Curie*, Kurt Beyer's *Grace Hopper*, Edmund Morris's *Edison*, James Essinger's *A Female Genius*. Numerical figures are best-current-consensus values.
